# Methods — source of truth

The manuscript Methods are generated as a Word document by **`scripts/110_build_methods_docx.py`**
→ `Reports/SP_TB_Manuscript_Methods.docx` (Drive). Edit the prose in script 110 and re-run to
regenerate the .docx, so the text and the numbers stay in one place.

Study-population flow and the geocoding hierarchy are documented in [PIPELINE.md](PIPELINE.md).
Key figures (rebuilt authoritative base, scripts 108/109/100):

- 179,925 incident (new + relapse) notifications eligible for geocoding
- 179,553 geocoded (99.8%); 176,127 assigned to a ≥10-case region for analysis
- 8,717 TB deaths; 13,059 treatment abandonments (8.4% of 156,390 evaluated)
- crude statewide adult notification rate 40.8 / 100,000 / year
- 7,314 regions; 5,083 with ≥10 pooled cases
