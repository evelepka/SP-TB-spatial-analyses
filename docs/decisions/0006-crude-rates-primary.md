# ADR-0006 — Crude rates are the primary basis; age-standardisation is a sensitivity analysis

## Context
The study question is programmatic: where are the cases, deaths and treatment losses? A region
with many cases because of its age structure still needs the services. Age-standardisation
answers a comparative question (risk net of age structure) and would add a second rate system
to the Methods.

On the same regions, crude and age-standardised top-20% hotspot sets overlap closely (Jaccard
0.97 notifications, 0.93 mortality, 0.83 LTFU). Concentration shares and Gini coefficients are
identical for notifications and mortality. The one material difference is the deprivation
excess fraction for mortality (28% crude versus 35% age-standardised): deprived regions are
younger and TB mortality rises with age, so crude rates understate the mortality gradient, a
conservative direction.

## Decision
1. `scripts/rank_basis.py` is the single switch. `RANK=crude` (default): notifications and
   mortality per adult population, LTFU as the crude proportion of evaluated episodes; hotspot
   flags in `region_units.csv` are built on this basis. `RANK=std` regenerates every figure with
   the suffix `_std` for the supplement; `RANK=percap` reproduces per-capita LTFU
   (supplementary figure on per-capita LTFU).
2. The mixed-effects model of LTFU (script 121) is the explanatory analysis, not a hotspot
   definition. It is reported as the predicted LTFU range between the 10th and 90th percentile
   regions and the proportional change in between-region variance after adjustment, rather than
   as median odds ratios (the median odds ratio is kept as an internal consistency check in
   `test/artifact_pins.json`).
3. Supplement: deprivation-quintile figures regenerated by script 129 on the crude basis; the
   age-standardised version of Figure 3 is included because it is the one place where the two
   bases differ materially.

## Consequences
Scripts 101, 123 and 124 write suffixed outputs; script 116 consumes the `_std` and `_percap`
variants for the supplement.
