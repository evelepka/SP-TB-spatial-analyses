# ADR-0006 — Crude rates are the primary basis for hotspots; age-standardization is a sensitivity analysis

- **Status:** accepted (2026-09-05, Jason + Evelyn, after the colleagues' "Methods too dense" feedback)
- **Supersedes:** the 2026-08-11 decision "standardize everything, note robustness" (recorded in
  scripts 102/105/122 headers and ADR-0005's ranking basis `RANK=std`). ADR-0005's estimand
  (LTFU = proportion among evaluated episodes) stands; only the age adjustment of that
  proportion moves to the sensitivity analysis.

## Context

The paper's question is programmatic — *where are the cases, deaths and treatment losses?* —
and a region with many cases because its population is old (or young) still needs the
services. Age-standardization answers a different, comparative question (risk net of age
structure). Keeping standardized rates primary forced a second rate system into an already
dense Methods section, and the hybrid alternative (crude for targeting, standardized for the
inequality inference) was judged more complexity for one number.

Measured on the same regions (2026-09-05): crude-ranked vs age-standardized top-20% hotspot
sets Jaccard 0.97 (incidence), 0.93 (mortality), 0.83 (LTFU). Concentration shares and Gini
identical for incidence/mortality; LTFU 24%/0.23 crude vs 22%/0.21 standardized. Deprivation
excess fraction: incidence 34% vs 33%; **mortality 28% vs 35%** — the only material difference,
in the conservative direction (deprived regions are younger and TB mortality rises with age,
so crude rates understate the mortality gradient). LTFU acquires a weak gradient under crude
rates (d ≤ 0.20 vs ≤ 0.12).

## Decision

1. `scripts/rank_basis.py` is the single switch. `RANK=crude` (default) — incidence and
   mortality per adult population, LTFU as the crude proportion of evaluated episodes;
   hotspot flags in `region_units.csv` are built on this basis and this is what
   `Data/analytic/region_units.csv` persists. `RANK=std` regenerates every figure with
   suffix `_std` for the supplement; `RANK=percap` reproduces the historical per-capita LTFU
   (Supplementary Figure S8 only).
2. The GLMM (script 121) stays — it is the explanatory analysis, not a hotspot definition —
   but is reported as the predicted-LTFU range between the 10th and 90th percentile regions
   (≈8% to 19%) and the proportional change in between-region variance (0% for case-mix, 4%
   for supervised treatment), not as median odds ratios in the main text. The latent-scale
   ICC (4%) is NOT reported as "variance explained" (see dead-ends).
3. Supplement: S5–S7 regenerated by script 129 on the crude basis; S8 = per-capita LTFU
   (finding kept from ADR-0005); S9 = age-standardized Figure 3 (vulnerability associations),
   the one place where the two bases differ materially.
